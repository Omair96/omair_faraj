About me

About

I am a full-time faculty member in Computer and Communications Engineering at the Lebanese International University (LIU). I hold a Ph.D. (Excellent Cum Laude) from an international cotutelle between Institut Polytechnique de Paris and Universitat Oberta de Catalunya.

My work spans network security, ML-based intrusion detection, IoT security, zero-watermarking, data provenance and cryptography. It has appeared in Q1 venues including ACM Computing Surveys (IF 23.8) and the Journal of Information Security and Applications.

I have taught at LIU, Beirut Arab University and ISSAE–CNAM Liban. That work includes coordinating courses and supervising senior projects and master's theses. I also review for IEEE Internet of Things Journal, Elsevier Computer Networks, ESORICS and other venues.
